Our Story

Mbaki House is situated along Nyeri’s bustling Gakere Road — named after the pre-colonial chief Gakere wa Ngunju. Even though Gakere Road is one of the town’s busiest commercial arteries, Mbaki House Hub offers a calm, inviting space to work, collaborate and build.

We chose this location for convenience: there’s ample parking and quick access to restaurants, banks, and other essential services. Our members enjoy reliable Wi-Fi, power backup, and a community of serious professionals who value focus and connection.
